Welcome to Kenyatta University

Dear Student,

Whether you are beginning First Year with a new admission letter still smelling of fresh ink, or you are a Fourth Year finishing the final units before graduation, this portal was built for you. It brings together revision materials, past papers, unit outlines, teaching-subject combinations, and answers to the questions every School of Education student asks at least once — all organised by year and semester, so you spend less time searching and more time studying.

Kenyatta University has, since 1985, stood for one thing above all: Elimu ni Nguvu — Education is strength. Carry that with you through every lecture, every teaching practice, every exam season. You are capable of the work ahead.

If you ever get stuck — a broken link, a missing unit, or a question this site doesn't yet answer — tap "Report an Issue" above and it will take you straight to my inbox.

Welcome to Kenyatta University. SNR. OHANGA MAXWELL ODHIAMBO — 10th Delegate, School of Education and Lifelong Learning, 2027/2028
